## SelTranslate 0.0.8

- **Who spoke when.** A new Speakers button on a History entry finds the speakers in the recording (up to 4) and transcribes each reply on its own, with a time stamp: `[00:01:23] Speaker 2: …`. Copy the result or save it as a text file. The speaker model (NVIDIA Sortformer, Q8_0 / F16 / F32, 139 to 471 MB) downloads on first use and runs on the processor.
- **Language per file.** Speakers lets you pick the language of the recording, so short replies are not misheard in another language. Your Settings stay as they are.
- **Cleaner footer.** The update status is gone: SelTranslate has no update feed, so it only ever said "Update Checking Disabled". The version is still there.
